# Regional — other (Jais, ALLaM, K2, SEA-LION, HyperCLOVA X, Upstage Solar)

Six regional/sovereign-adjacent families with enough verified material to be useful but not enough individually to justify a standalone profile: **Jais 2** (UAE — Inception / G42 / MBZUAI), **ALLaM** (Saudi Arabia — SDAIA / HUMAIN), **K2** (UAE — MBZUAI / IFM, K2-Think and its successor K2-Horizon), **SEA-LION** (Singapore — AI Singapore), **HyperCLOVA X** (Korea — NAVER), and **Upstage Solar** (Korea).

**Sailor2 is intentionally not covered here.** It is a separate project from AI Singapore's SEA-LION; no vendor-owned page (`aisingapore` HF org or `sea-lion.ai`) publishes Sailor2 content in the source research this profile is built from, so nothing is recorded for it. Do not conflate it with SEA-LION or reintroduce it from memory.

**ALLaM has only two primary-provider sources** (below the three-link bar this skill otherwise expects per family) — flagged explicitly rather than padded with unrelated links. Its HuggingFace org page, `ALLaM-AI`, **404s**; the model itself still resolves under the `humain-ai` org, which is the canonical location used throughout this file.

The eleven headings below describe what is **common across the group**; per-model deltas — which is where most of the load-bearing detail actually lives — are in the `## <Model>` subsections that follow.

### API surface

No family in this group publishes a first-party, vendor-hosted production API comparable to a frontier provider's, with two exceptions: **SEA-LION** (`https://api.sea-lion.ai/v1`, OpenAI-compatible shape, rate-limited to 10 requests/minute/user) and **Upstage** (`https://api.upstage.ai/v1`, explicitly "OpenAI SDK compatible" — the clearest such claim in this group). **HyperCLOVA X** has a proprietary hosted surface (`clovastudio.stream.ntruss.com/v3/chat-completions/{modelName}`) that is **not** OpenAI-compatible. **Jais, ALLaM, and K2** have no documented vendor API at all — only demo chat UIs (`jaischat.ai`, `chat.ifm.ai`) or self-serve weights. Confirm which surface a detected model ID maps to before assuming OpenAI-compatible request/response shapes.

### Reasoning state

Only **K2-Horizon** documents an explicit reasoning contract: recommended `reasoning_effort="high"`, `temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95`, with a dedicated reasoning parser. **HyperCLOVA X** gates thinking/reasoning mode to `HCX-007` only. No reasoning-echo requirement (passing prior reasoning state back across turns) is documented for any of the six — treat as an open question per model, not a confirmed absence.

### Tool semantics

**This is the sharpest capability split in the group.** Documented: SEA-LION (Qwen-SEA-LION-v4.5, framed for agentic loops), K2-Horizon (first-class — dedicated `k2_horizon` tool-call parser plus `--enable-auto-tool-choice`, the strongest documented agent contract of any regional family in this research pass), Upstage Solar (up to 128 tools per request, standard OpenAI function-calling interface), HyperCLOVA X (model-gated: `HCX-007` and `HCX-005` only, and only when `maxTokens` exceeds 1024). **Not documented at all: Jais-2 and ALLaM** — both bilingual Arabic models, and both short-context (8K and 4K tokens respectively). That combination makes Jais-2 and ALLaM poor fits for agent/tool-calling harnesses regardless of language quality — it is a capability boundary, not a preference to work around with prompting.

### Modality support

All six are text-first; only HyperCLOVA X's open-weight `SEED-Think-32B` and SEA-LION's Gemma v4.5 variant claim broader multimodal input (images/video, and audio for the Gemma variant). **Language and script coverage per model is a capability boundary, not a marketing line — see each `## <Model>` subsection for the named list.** Do not infer a family's language coverage from its country of origin: K2 is a UAE/MBZUAI program but its cards declare **English only**, with no Arabic capability claimed anywhere — that is Jais and Falcon territory, not K2's.

### Context behavior

Wide spread: ALLaM 4,096 tokens (tightest in this survey — rules out most long-context agent patterns), Jais-2 8,192 tokens (short by 2026 standards), HyperCLOVA X SEED-Think-32B 128K, Upstage `solar-mini` 32K up to `solar-pro4` 512K (1M for the open-weight `Solar-Open2-250B`), SEA-LION Gemma v4.5 128K / Qwen v4.5 262K, K2-Horizon 524,288 tokens (512K, the largest in the group; K2-Think's own context is not stated on its card).

### Structured output path

Documented for **Upstage Solar** (`response_format` with `json_schema`; first-level properties limited to `string`/`integer`/`number`/`array`, max nesting depth 10) and **HyperCLOVA X** (structured outputs, but gated to `HCX-007` only). Not documented for Jais-2, ALLaM, K2 (either generation), or SEA-LION.

### Deployment & residency

This is the load-bearing section for every model in this file, and the group's own cross-cutting finding is blunt: **"sovereign" branding rarely comes with a documented residency guarantee.** Across all six models, only **Upstage** documents anything concrete for its managed surface — "dedicated and on-premises deployment" plus SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified operations, though **no country or region is named** even there. Every other model in this file reduces to "self-host the open weights wherever you like":

- Jais-2, ALLaM, and both K2 generations ship **open weights (Apache 2.0)** with self-hosting as the only documented path, and **no data-residency, hosting-region, or sovereign-cloud guarantee on any reachable vendor page** for any of the three. `inception42.ai` and `ifm.ai` both returned HTTP 403 during this research pass.
- SEA-LION's hosted `api.sea-lion.ai` inference location is **not documented** — no country, region, or provider is named, and the docs direct residency questions to an email address rather than a published policy.
- HyperCLOVA X is hosted via CLOVA Studio on NAVER Cloud Platform, with a separate public-institutions tier (`clovastudio.gov-ncloud.com`), but **no explicit region, data-residency, or sovereignty guarantee is stated** in the reachable CLOVA Studio docs or product pages.
- **No family in this file documents a region for its own managed inference endpoint.** Regional provenance and data residency are independent properties here — a UAE- or Korea-headquartered vendor does not thereby guarantee UAE- or Korea-hosted inference. Where a downstream system has a residency obligation, that obligation is unmet by any of these six models' hosted surfaces as of 2026-09-08; only self-hosting the open weights onto infrastructure the operator controls closes that gap.

### Known production failure modes

- **Jais-2 and ALLaM: agent runtime semantics not publicly documented as of 2026-09-08; validate tool-calling and structured output against the deployment before production use.** Both cards are silent on function calling and structured output, and both have short contexts (8K / 4K) that constrain agent patterns regardless.
- Assuming K2's UAE/MBZUAI origin implies Arabic capability — the K2-Think and K2-Horizon cards declare English only.
- Assuming SEA-LION's or K2-Horizon's base-model breadth (e.g. "201 languages" inherited from Qwen) reflects fine-tuned regional quality — that is base-model breadth, not the family's own evaluated coverage; use the named fine-tuning language list instead.
- Building against SEA-LION's public API without accounting for its 10-requests-per-minute-per-user limit — it is not a production-throughput surface.
- Assuming HyperCLOVA X's `HCX-*` line is OpenAI-compatible — no compatibility is claimed anywhere in the NAVER Cloud docs, and parameter names differ (`topP`, `topK`, `maxTokens`).
- Fine-tuning a HyperCLOVA X model and expecting the tuned model to retain function calling or structured outputs — tuning-trained jobs lose image input, inference of certain kinds, function calling, and structured outputs.
- Treating any of these six models' country of origin as a data-residency guarantee for its managed API — none of the six documents a hosting region for its own managed endpoint.

### Harness requirements

- Route Jais-2 and ALLaM only to non-agentic, short-context conversational use cases until the deploying team independently validates tool-calling and structured-output behavior against the actual serving stack.
- For K2-Horizon, pin the `k2_horizon` tool-call parser and `--enable-auto-tool-choice`, and set `reasoning_effort`, `temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95` per vendor guidance rather than a generic default.
- For HyperCLOVA X, gate function-calling and structured-output routing to `HCX-007`/`HCX-005` specifically, and never route a fine-tuned HyperCLOVA X job through a tool-calling or structured-output path.
- For Upstage Solar, cap tool definitions at 128 per request and validate function-name characters (alphanumeric plus underscore/hyphen, max 64 chars) before sending.
- If a data-residency obligation applies to any of the six, require self-hosting the open weights (Jais-2, ALLaM, K2, or the applicable SEA-LION/HyperCLOVA-SEED open-weight releases) on infrastructure the operator controls — do not rely on any of these six's managed API for a residency guarantee. Upstage's dedicated/on-prem offering is the nearest exception and should still be confirmed for the required region directly with the vendor before relying on it.

### Retired / migration targets

- Jais: not publicly documented as of 2026-09-08; Jais-1 repos remain published with no deprecation notice, though Jais-2 is presented as the successor generation.
- ALLaM: not publicly documented as of 2026-09-08; `v1` remains fetchable alongside `v2`.
- K2: not publicly documented; `IFM/K2-Think` carries no deprecation marker and names no successor, even though K2-Horizon is clearly the active line — the succession is inferable from release activity, not stated.
- SEA-LION: not documented in the sources reviewed for this pass.
- HyperCLOVA X: not documented in the sources reviewed for this pass.
- Upstage publishes the most complete lifecycle table of any family in this group, at `/docs/models/history` — see the Upstage Solar subsection below for dated retirements.

### Re-evaluate when

- Any of the 403-blocked pages (`inception42.ai`, `ifm.ai`) or the 404'd `ALLaM-AI` HF org becomes reachable — residency and organizational-identity claims for Jais, K2, and ALLaM should be re-sourced directly rather than continuing to treat them as unknown.
- A vendor-hosted, production-grade API is announced for Jais, ALLaM, or either K2 generation — none exists today.
- SEA-LION's `api.sea-lion.ai` hosting location or rate limit changes, or AI Singapore publishes a residency policy in place of the email-based referral.
- NAVER publishes a region or data-residency statement for CLOVA Studio, or extends OpenAI-compatibility to the `HCX-*` line.
- Sailor2 publishes a vendor-owned page — at that point it would warrant its own evaluation rather than continued exclusion from this file.

## Jais 2 (UAE — Inception / G42 / MBZUAI)

**Namespace: the HuggingFace org is now `inception42`.** The `inceptionai` namespace still resolves for individual model URLs, but its org page states it is "not operational and only created to reserve the… user name." `www.inceptionai.ai` 301-redirects to `inception42.ai`, which returned HTTP 403 in this research pass.

- **Current models:** `inception42/Jais-2-70B-Chat` (72B) and `inception42/Jais-2-8B-Chat` (8B), plus `-GGUF` variants. Earlier generations remain published: `jais-adapted-70b-chat-4bit-bnb`, `jais-family-256m`, `jais-family-256m-chat`, `jais-30b-chat-v3`, `jais-30b-v3`, and the wider `jais-family-*` / `jais-adapted-*` releases (590M–70B). Also `inception42/Llama-3.1-Sherkala-8B-Chat`, a Kazakh-focused sibling release — not Jais. Developed jointly by MBZUAI, Inception, and Cerebras.
- **Language & script:** **Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic and regional dialects) and English.** Bilingual by design, Arabic-centric. Scripts: Arabic and Latin, implied by the language pair. The cards do not discuss transliteration, Arabizi / romanized Arabic, or per-dialect script handling, and do not enumerate which dialects. Vocabulary size 150,272 is the only quantitative tokenizer signal given.
- **Runtime contract delta:** context length **8,192 tokens** for both Jais-2 chat models. Transformer decoder-only with RoPE, squared-ReLU, custom μP parameterization. Tool/function calling is **not documented at all** on the Jais-2 cards — do not assume it works. Structured output not documented. Text only. Serving: transformers, vLLM, SGLang, Docker. A hosted chat experience runs on Cerebras hardware at `jaischat.ai` — a chat UI, not a documented API. Model files are gated on HuggingFace.
- **Deployment & residency delta:** open weights under Apache 2.0; self-hosting is the documented path; no data-residency, UAE-hosting, or sovereign-cloud guarantee is documented on any reachable vendor page.

## SEA-LION (Singapore — AI Singapore)

Structured output not documented.
- **Deployment & residency delta:** Apache-2.0 for both v4.5 models per their cards; the docs' general statement is that SEA-LION aims at MIT "as much as possible" but per-model terms follow the base model (Llama3, Gemma, Qwen) — check the individual card, do not assume MIT. Self-hosting is extensively documented: vLLM on Linux, Ollama, Kaggle, plus Google Vertex AI, Amazon Bedrock Custom Model Import, a Bedrock Access Gateway, and Cloudflare Workers AI. **Where the hosted `api.sea-lion.ai` inference runs is not documented** — no country, region, or provider is named, and no data-residency or Singapore-sovereignty guarantee appears; the docs direct residency questions to an email address. Governance context that is stated: SEA-LION is anchored by the Products Pillar of AI Singapore, supported by the National Research Foundation and NUS.
